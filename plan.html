EVERY SESSION · BEFORE MAIN WORKOUT · 10 MINUTES

Lower Back Protocol

Dr. Stuart McGill's Big 3 + glute & core support. Evidence-based spine stabilization.

McGILL BIG 3 + SUPPORT WORK — PERFORM BEFORE EVERY SESSION

Cat-Camel
10 slow cycles · 3 sec each end position · Spine mobility warm-up · Zero pain = correct pace
McGill Curl-Up
5×10s hold · 3×10s · 1×10s · Hands under lumbar curve · Lift only head/shoulders — no lumbar flexion
Bird Dog
5×10s ea side · 3×10s · 1×10s · All fours, opposite arm + leg · Hips level · Back neutral
McGill Side Plank
5×10s ea side · 3×10s · 1×10s · Start with knees bent · Progress to feet when ready
Glute Bridge
3 × 12–15 reps · Drive through heels · Squeeze glutes at top · Don't hyperextend spine
Dead Bug
3 × 8 each side · Low back pressed into floor entire time · 3 sec controlled descent · Core anti-extension

⚠ Stop any movement that causes sharp or radiating pain. Consult a physio if pain is acute or worsening. Russian Twists removed from plan — high lumbar shear. Goblet squat replaces barbell squat weeks 1–3.

LDL-LOWERING FOODS — EAT MORE

  • Oats, oat bran, barley (soluble fiber)
  • Salmon, sardines, walnuts (omega-3)
  • Broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts (DIM — lowers estrogen → reduces chest fat)
  • Avocado, olive oil, almonds (monounsaturated fat)
  • Blueberries, strawberries (antioxidants)
  • Black beans, lentils, chickpeas (fiber + plant protein)
  • Ground flaxseed 1–2 tbsp daily (lignans reduce estrogen)

LIMIT OR AVOID

  • Alcohol — converts to estrogen in liver, promotes chest fat
  • Refined carbs and added sugar (raise triglycerides)
  • Processed meats and fried foods
  • Trans fats — directly raise LDL
  • Full-fat dairy frequently
